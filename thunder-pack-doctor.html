THUNDER PACK DOCTOR

Finds and repairs the resource-pack problems that turn fire, lava, water, portals and other animated textures into the purple/black missing texture. Everything runs in your browser; the pack is never uploaded.

    What it fixes: block/item textures that are animation strips (taller than wide) need a matching <name>.png.mcmeta in the same pack, and every frame listed in it must exist - otherwise the game rejects the texture. Missing files are added (with vanilla frame order and speed when the frame count matches), bad frame numbers are removed, <name>.mcmeta is renamed to <name>.png.mcmeta, and broken JSON is replaced. Textures wider than tall are only reported, because they need to be resized by hand.
